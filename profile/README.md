## Who we are

**Aurica Circular** is the trading name of **Aurica Co., Ltd. (歐瑞卡有限公司)**, a Taiwanese company
registered in Taichung in March 2026.

We don't ask people to be perfect: we make the good practice simple, measurable and rewarding —
whether it's handling food waste where people live, or keeping your documents on your own machine.

## 🧠 Software

### [AiTao](https://github.com/Aurica-Circular/AiTao) — your private, local AI document assistant

Search and chat over your own files, in any language — **entirely on your computer**.
No cloud, no data leaks, no usage analytics.

- **Core** — free and open source (AGPL-3.0): text files and text-based PDFs, semantic search, grounded chat
- **Premium** — Office formats and advanced OCR (scans, images, tables), optimised for Traditional Chinese, French and English — [licence on request](https://auricacircular.com/en/contact/?besoin=aitao)
- Runs on macOS and Windows 11, with Ollama or llama.cpp

`local-first` `privacy` `rag` `ocr` `meilisearch` `ollama`

## 🌱 How we build

- **Privacy by design** — your data stays on your machine
- **Frugality** — software that runs on hardware you already own
- **Open source first** — we build on open components and publish our core
- **Mutual aid, frugality, fairness** — a business model that doesn't betray its values
